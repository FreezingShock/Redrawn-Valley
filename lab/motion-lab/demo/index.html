1. Opening drawing

What plays on a first visit and every reload: the logo draws, the wordmark writes, the sheet lifts in tiles. A tap skips ahead.

2. Tile wipe (fallback)

What plays when you follow a link in a browser without page transitions. Pick where you “click”; the tiles grow from that point and lift from it on the next page.

3. Heading write-on

Letters ink in one by one, bouncing on the Wobble axes, then a pencil line draws under the title.

4. Pencil dab

Every click or tap on the site leaves a quick hand-drawn burst. Try the grid.

5. Panel zoom

On the real site, clicking a panel makes the browser grow its picture to fill the screen, then lift it to show the new page. This is the version for browsers without that feature: the panel’s own picture grows while the next page loads. Press the panel.

6. Anchor jump

In-page links glide on one easing curve instead of snapping, then the heading you land on gets an ink sweep.
