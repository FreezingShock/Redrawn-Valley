What this guide is, and is not

I am a student, not a hydrologist or an arborist. These entries summarize government pages, peer-reviewed abstracts, case study briefs and news reports I read myself, 22 sources in all, and say what I could not verify. Where I give my own reasoning or arithmetic, it is labeled. Wikipedia is used only for basic geography and flagged each time. If something here is wrong, I want to know.
