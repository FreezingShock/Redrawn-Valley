How I write these

Every number has a source

The same rules as the studies, applied to prose.

Cited, or labeled

A number either has a numbered source at the bottom or is marked as my own estimate or assumption.

Limits stated

Each note ends with what I could not verify, including where I only read an abstract or a search excerpt.

Concept, not commission

These are a student’s notes, not engineering advice. Nothing here is a design for a real project.

See a mistake? Tell me and I will fix it and say what changed.
