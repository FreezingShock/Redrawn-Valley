Every page gets its own drawing

The banners across this site are generated from a seed, so each page and each note keeps the same composition every time. Pick a seed, reroll, and watch a tile change its colour, shape or icon every few seconds. Read how it was made →

The set

Fourteen motifs, each one thing

Borrowed from the pattern, not the pictures: flat tiles on a single field, grids on the shape and never on the field, drafting tools as objects, and constant line weight. Every one is drawn in code in our palette.

Palette

Two to four colours, plus ink and paper

The art accents are for decoration only. They never carry text or meaning; brick, moss and slate keep their jobs in the interface.

  • One saturated or pale field per tile; a field colour is not reused inside one banner when it can be avoided.
  • The grid sits on the shape, not the field. Line weight stays the same within one drawing.
  • Motion is slow and rare: one tile shifts every five to ten seconds, only while the art is on screen. With reduced motion set, nothing moves.
  • Same seed, same drawing. Use the page slug as the seed.
