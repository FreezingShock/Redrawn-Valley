How this stays current

Nothing here is typed by hand

When I publish a page with numbered notes, the check script reads them and rebuilds this list. There is nothing to remember to update.

Reads the notes

scripts/build_sources.py finds every numbered note and every marker that points at it, in guides, notes, studies and lab write-ups.

Merges by link

The same article cited on three pages becomes one source with three places of use. Tracking tags and trailing slashes are ignored.

Keeps the sentence

Each use stores the sentence the marker closes and its section, so the library can show how a source is used without opening the page.

Marks the exact spot

A jump link carries the marker’s order on the page. The page scrolls there and highlights the sentence. Open a source from here to see it.

Limits: types and topics are assigned by rules (the publisher’s web address and the words on the citing page), so a few labels will be off. A caveat is whatever I wrote next to the source, such as “seen only as a search excerpt.” I do not rate sources here.
